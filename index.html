How the conversion works

The whole-number portion becomes degrees. The decimal portion is multiplied by 60 and rounded to the nearest whole minute. Minutes are always shown with two digits, followed by N/S or E/W. If rounding produces 60 minutes, the app carries it into the next degree.

Runs entirely in your browser. No coordinates are uploaded or stored.
